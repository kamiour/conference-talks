## About the Talk

Platform tooling is essential for scaling engineering organizations, but building robust platform solutions takes time while product deadlines don't wait. 

This talk covers **Forward-Adoption** — a strategy for building on future platform directions before official abstractions are ready, keeping delivery on schedule while minimizing long-term migration costs.

### Three Approaches:
1. **Use the Future Foundation Directly** — Adopting third-party libraries chosen by the platform team early.
2. **Strangler Fig Behind a Facade** — Gradual, safe surface-by-surface migration behind a stable interface.
3. **Ports & Adapters (Hexagonal Architecture)** — Decoupling core business logic from volatile integration APIs.

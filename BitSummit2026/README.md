## About the Talk

How product teams adopt internal platform tooling at Netflix scale — real-life architecture cases, migration failure modes, and practical takeaways.

### Key Highlights:
- **UI Paved Road (UIPR)**: The two pillars of Netflix UI Platform — Web Framework and Hawkins Design System.
- **Migration Strategies**:
  - Why big bang migration failed under its own weight, and how a phased approach cut migration time from 2 months to under a week per app.
  - Using future foundations directly (e.g. adopting AG Grid ahead of the platform wrapper `hawkins-table-next`).
  - Decoupling core business rules with **Ports & Adapters (Hexagonal Architecture)** in React.
- **AI Agent Automation**: Codifying migration runbooks into executable AI agent skills.
- **Key Learnings**: Align direction instead of waiting for platform timing, isolate volatile integrations, partner closely with platform teams, and leave runnable documentation.

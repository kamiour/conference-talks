## About the Talk

How product teams adopt internal platform tooling at Netflix scale — real-life examples, failure modes, and practical takeaways.

### Key Highlights:
- **UI Paved Road (UIPR)**: The two pillars of Netflix UI Platform — Web Framework and Hawkins Design System.
- **Big Bang vs. Phased Migration**: Why the big bang migration failed under its own weight, and how a phased approach with runbooks cut migration time from 2 months down to under a week per app.
- **AI Agent Automation**: Turning migration runbooks and documentation into an AI agent skill to automate execution end-to-end.
- **Key Learnings**: Divide and conquer, fail fast and adapt, partner closely with platform teams, and leave runnable documentation.
